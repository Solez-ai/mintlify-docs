---

Get a BrailleBridge BB1 unit connected to a student device and a teacher console in under five minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Power on the BB1 hardware (Wiring Board v4)">
    Connect your battery pack (`4×AA` alkaline on `JP1 1-2` bypass, or `3–16 V` NiMH/Li-ion/9V/DC supply through the `J5` 5 V buck-boost module on `JP1 2-3`) and flip the slide power switch **ON** (or plug a Micro-USB cable into the 38-pin ESP32 DevKit).

    - On **BB1 Wiring Board v4**, `D2` (`1N5819`) protects the buck-boost module against reverse polarity and `D1` (`1N5819`) isolates USB 5 V from back-feeding into the battery rail before feeding ESP32 `V5`/`VIN` (`J2/19`).
    - On boot, the firmware prints `SYSTEM: BrailleBridge Connected. Waiting for BLE...` on USB Serial (`115200` baud) and begins BLE advertising (`BrailleBridge`, `BrailleBridge 2`, `BrailleBridge 3`, etc.).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect via Bluetooth on Student or Teacher device">
    Launch the Desktop Teacher Suite or Android Companion App — no manual Windows Settings pairing is required for BLE NUS classroom streaming.

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="PyQt6 Desktop Suite (Recommended)">
        ```bash
        cd braille-desktop
        pip install -r requirements.txt
        python main.py
        ```
        - In **Student View**, select **BLE: BrailleBridge** (or **USB Serial** / **Bluetooth HID Keyboard**) and type directly onto the permanent 22 pt Microsoft Word–style document canvas with 100%–300% boosted audio readback.
        - In **Teacher View**, click **Scan BLE** (which automatically powers on the Windows Bluetooth radio if off), enter a student name, select their ESP32 (`BrailleBridge 2`, `BrailleBridge 3`, etc.), and click **`+ Add Student`**. The app auto-connects immediately, sends `SYSTEM:START`, opens the student's Word document tab, and auto-advances the selector to the next ESP32.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Android Companion App">
        Open **BrailleBridge** on Android 7.0+ (`minSdk 24`), grant Bluetooth Scan/Connect permissions, and tap **Add Student** or **Scan BLE** in **Teacher** mode. `BleProxyService` maintains concurrent GATT connections in the background.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Website Live Demo">
        Open [solez.vercel.app](https://solez.vercel.app) in Chrome or Edge, scroll to the **Live Interactive Pad**, and click **Connect BLE** (`navigator.bluetooth`) or **Connect USB** (`navigator.serial`).
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Type your first chord">
    Press **Dot 1** (top-left) and **Dot 4** (top-right) together, then release both keys.

    - The character **`c`** appears immediately in the active feed (and via BLE HID on any focused text field).
    - **Tap Shift** (`25–1500 ms` with no dots) to send **Backspace**.
    - **Hold Shift + Chord** for digits (`1–0`), symbols (`+ - × = ÷ , ; : .`), uppercase letters, or Bangla vowel signs (matras).
    - **Hold Dots 4+5+6 for 500 ms** to toggle between **English** (`LANG:en`) and **Bangla** (`LANG:bn`).
  </Step>
</Steps>
