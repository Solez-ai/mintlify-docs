---

Get a BrailleBridge BB1 unit connected to a student device and a teacher console in under five minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Power on the BB1 hardware">
    Insert `4×AA` batteries into the battery pack and flip the slide power switch **ON** (or plug a Micro-USB cable into the 38-pin ESP32 DevKit).

    - Battery `+` routes through the slide switch and `1N5819` Schottky diode (`D1`) into the ESP32 `V5`/`VIN` pin.
    - On boot, the firmware prints `SYSTEM: BrailleBridge Connected. Waiting for BLE...` on USB Serial (`115200` baud) and begins BLE advertising as **`BrailleBridge`**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pair via Bluetooth on Student or Teacher device">
    Open Bluetooth settings on Windows, macOS, Android, or iOS and pair with **`BrailleBridge`**.

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="PyQt6 Desktop App (Recommended)">
        ```bash
        cd braille-desktop
        pip install -r requirements.txt
        python main.py
        ```
        - In **Student View**, select **BLE: BrailleBridge** (or **USB Serial** / **Bluetooth HID Keyboard**).
        - In **Teacher View**, click **Add Student**, choose **Bluetooth (BLE)**, select each student's BrailleBridge unit, and click **Connect**. Multiple bridges connect simultaneously.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Android Companion App">
        Open **BrailleBridge** on Android 7.0+ (`minSdk 24`), grant Bluetooth Scan/Connect permissions, and tap **Add Student** or **Scan BLE** in **Teacher** mode. `BleProxyService` maintains concurrent GATT connections in the background.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Website Live Demo">
        Open [solez.vercel.app](https://solez.vercel.app) in Chrome or Edge, scroll to the **Live Interactive Pad**, and click **Connect BLE** (`navigator.bluetooth`) or **Connect USB** (`navigator.serial`).
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Type your first chord">
    Press **Dot 1** (top-left) and **Dot 4** (top-right) together, then release both keys.

    - The character **`c`** appears immediately in the active feed (and via BLE HID on any focused text field).
    - **Tap Shift** (`25–1500 ms` with no dots) to send **Backspace**.
    - **Hold Shift + Chord** for digits (`1–0`), symbols (`+ - × = ÷ , ; : .`), uppercase letters, or Bangla vowel signs (matras).
    - **Hold Dots 4+5+6 for 500 ms** to toggle between **English** (`LANG:en`) and **Bangla** (`LANG:bn`).
  </Step>
</Steps>
