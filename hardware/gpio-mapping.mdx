All eight mechanical switches connect between an ESP32 GPIO pin and the shared `GND` plane. In `braile/braile.ino`, every channel is initialized with `INPUT_PULLUP` (`HIGH` when idle, `LOW` when pressed).

## Pin Assignment Table

| Channel | Firmware Constant | ESP32 GPIO | Bitmask | `J1` Header Pin | `J2` Socket Pin | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Dot 1** | `DOT_1` | `14` | `0b000001` | `J1/1` (`S`) | `J2/12` | Left column, top |
| **Dot 2** | `DOT_2` | `12*` | `0b000010` | `J1/3` (`S`) | `J2/13` | **MTDI boot-strapping pin** — no external pull-up |
| **Dot 3** | `DOT_3` | `13` | `0b000100` | `J1/5` (`S`) | `J2/15` | Left column, bottom |
| **Dot 4** | `DOT_4` | `27` | `0b001000` | `J1/7` (`S`) | `J2/11` | Right column, top |
| **Dot 5** | `DOT_5` | `26` | `0b010000` | `J1/9` (`S`) | `J2/10` | Right column, middle |
| **Dot 6** | `DOT_6` | `25` | `0b100000` | `J1/11` (`S`) | `J2/9` | Right column, bottom / Capital sign |
| **Space** | `SPACE` | `33` | — | `J1/13` (`S`) | `J2/8` | Inserts `' '` and clears `CAPS_ONCE` |
| **Shift** | `SHIFT` | `32` | — | `J1/15` (`S`) | `J2/7` | Hold = modifier; Tap (`25–1500 ms`) = Backspace |

<Warning>
  **GPIO12 (`DOT_2`) Boot-Strapping Rule:** On the ESP32-WROOM-32, `GPIO12` (`MTDI`) selects internal flash voltage at power-on (`LOW` = 3.3 V normal boot, `HIGH` = 1.8 V boot failure). Never solder an external pull-up resistor to `D2`. Because the Outemu Red switch is normally open and `pinMode(DOT_2, INPUT_PULLUP)` executes *after* boot in `setup()`, `GPIO12` boots reliably every time (just avoid holding Dot 2 down while flipping power on).
</Warning>

## Firmware Initialization (`braile/braile.ino`)

```cpp
#define DOT_1 14
#define DOT_2 12
#define DOT_3 13
#define DOT_4 27
#define DOT_5 26
#define DOT_6 25
#define SPACE 33
#define SHIFT 32

const unsigned long DEBOUNCE_MS = 15;
enum { P_D1, P_D2, P_D3, P_D4, P_D5, P_D6, P_SPACE, P_SHIFT, PIN_COUNT };
struct PinFilter { uint8_t pin; bool raw; bool stable; unsigned long lastChange; };

PinFilter filters[PIN_COUNT] = {
  {DOT_1, false, false, 0}, {DOT_2, false, false, 0}, {DOT_3, false, false, 0},
  {DOT_4, false, false, 0}, {DOT_5, false, false, 0}, {DOT_6, false, false, 0},
  {SPACE, false, false, 0}, {SHIFT, false, false, 0}
};
```
