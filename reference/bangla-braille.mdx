BrailleBridge's Bangla dictionary follows the **Bharati Braille** standard, which maps Braille cells to the *sounds* of South Asian scripts rather than to the Latin alphabet. Because of this phonetic mapping, many cells coincide between English and Bangla — pressing the same chord produces the phonetically related letter in each language. You do not need a different physical device or a different key layout; you simply switch the active dictionary by holding dots 4+5+6 for 500 ms, and the green LED confirms that Bangla mode is active. Everything on this page reflects the exact chords encoded in the BrailleBridge firmware.

## Vowels (স্বরবর্ণ)

Bangla has ten primary vowels. In Bangla mode you type each vowel as a standalone character — the independent vowel form (স্বরবর্ণ). To enter the dependent vowel sign (কার / মাত্রা) that attaches to a consonant, hold Shift while pressing the same vowel chord.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Vowels (স্বরবর্ণ)">
    Use these chords when you want to enter a vowel as a standalone letter — for example, at the start of a word.

    | Vowel | Romanisation | Dots | Binary |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | অ | O (short) | 1 | `0b000001` |
    | আ | Aa | 3, 4, 5 | `0b011100` |
    | ই | I | 2, 4 | `0b001010` |
    | ঈ | Long I | 3, 5 | `0b010100` |
    | উ | U | 1, 3, 6 | `0b100101` |
    | ঊ | Long U | 1, 2, 5, 6 | `0b110011` |
    | এ | E | 1, 5 | `0b010001` |
    | ঐ | Oi | 3, 4 | `0b001100` |
    | ও | O (long) | 1, 3, 5 | `0b010101` |
    | ঔ | Ou | 2, 4, 6 | `0b101010` |
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Vowel Signs / কার">
    In Bangla mode, pressing **Shift + vowel chord** produces the dependent vowel sign (কার / মাত্রা) for that vowel. Use these after typing a consonant to attach the vowel to it.

    | Vowel | Vowel Sign | Unicode | Entry Method |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | আ | া (Aa-kar) | U+09BE | Shift + আ chord |
    | ই | ি (I-kar) | U+09BF | Shift + ই chord |
    | ঈ | ী (Long I-kar) | U+09C0 | Shift + ঈ chord |
    | উ | ু (U-kar) | U+09C1 | Shift + উ chord |
    | ঊ | ূ (Long U-kar) | U+09C2 | Shift + ঊ chord |
    | এ | ে (E-kar) | U+09C7 | Shift + এ chord |
    | ঐ | ৈ (Oi-kar) | U+09C8 | Shift + ঐ chord |
    | ও | ো (O-kar) | U+09CB | Shift + ও chord |
    | ঔ | ৌ (Ou-kar) | U+09CC | Shift + ঔ chord |

    <Note>
    অ has no dependent vowel sign — it is the inherent vowel and is implied after every consonant unless overridden. Pressing Shift + অ chord has no effect and falls through to normal output.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Consonants (ব্যঞ্জনবর্ণ)

Bangla consonants are organised into traditional *varga* groups by their place of articulation, plus a final group of semi-vowels, sibilants, and the aspirate. Select the tab for the group you need.

  <Tab title="ক-বর্গ (Velar)">

    | Letter | Dots | Binary |
    |---|---|---|
    | ক | 1, 3 | `0b000101` |
    | খ | 4, 6 | `0b101000` |
    | গ | 1, 2, 4, 5 | `0b011011` |
    | ঘ | 1, 2, 6 | `0b100011` |
    | ঙ | 3, 4, 6 | `0b101100` |
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="চ-বর্গ (Palatal)">

    | Letter | Dots | Binary |

    | ছ | 1, 6 | `0b100001` |
    | জ | 2, 4, 5 | `0b011010` |

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="ট-বর্গ (Retroflex)">

## Special marks

These marks modify consonants and vowels rather than standing alone as full letters. You enter them as regular (unshifted) chords in Bangla mode.

| Mark | Name | Dots | Binary |
|---|---|---|---|
| ্ | Halant (হসন্ত / virama) | 4 | `0b001000` |
| ং | Anusvara (অনুস্বার) | 5, 6 | `0b110000` |
| ঃ | Visarga (বিসর্গ) | 6 | `0b100000` |
| ঁ | Candrabindu (চন্দ্রবিন্দু) | 5 | `0b010000` |

<Info>
The **halant** (্, dots 4 only) suppresses the inherent vowel sound of the consonant it follows. Type a consonant, then press the halant chord, then type the next consonant — this is how you form conjunct consonants (যুক্তাক্ষর). For example, to write **ক্ত** you enter ক → ্ → ত. The device transmits each character individually and your connected device assembles them into the correct conjunct glyph using the Unicode rendering engine.
</Info>
