## Teacher View (Multi-Student Grid)

In **Teacher** mode (`main.py`), every student in `roster.json` is rendered as a live card inside a responsive 2-column grid:

- **Add Student**: Choose **Bluetooth (BLE)**, **USB Serial**, or **Assign Later**, bind a device address/port, and start streaming immediately.
- **Live Card Controls**: View live translated text alongside a `MiniBrailleCell` showing the student's current chord, **Expand** into a large-type modal (`_open_expand_student_dialog`) for readability from across a desk, **Test** with simulated chord input, **Export**, **Clear**, or **Remove** the student.

## Logs View & `SmartFormatter`

The **Logs** view replaces the legacy Exam View and parses raw student transcripts through **`SmartFormatter`** (`protocol.py`) into structured visual blocks:

- `title`, `heading`, `question`, `answer`, `math`, `callout`, `bullet`, `numbered`, and `para`
- Toggle **Plain Text** view for raw character inspection
- Translate any student's log via Google Translate (`translate.googleapis.com/translate_a/single?client=gtx`)
- Export formatted transcripts to `{student}_formatted_log.txt` (or auto-saved `{name}_{YYYY-MM-DD_HH-MM-SS}.log` on disconnect)

## Speech Synthesis (`SpeechEngine`)

In `protocol.py`, `SpeechEngine.speak()` announces characters, symbols (`SYMBOL_SPEECH`), capital letters (`"capital A"`), and full words on Space in Learn Freeplay mode using Windows SAPI (`System.Speech.Synthesis.SpeechSynthesizer` at `$s.Rate = 1` via asynchronous PowerShell on `win32`).
